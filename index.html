The Rupture

Surprisingly, this was actually an incredibly fun experience! I really enjoyed playing this from start to finish despite its quirks, and it feels great to have a new hardest demon after just over a year. Being back on the list is great too—I wasn't expecting to make a comeback after Spectrum Cyclone fell off (and the subsequent motivation loss that followed).


I decided to go for this after a friend recommended it to me, and I ended up loving it, though there are some minor points of contention that brought the level down a little for me. It has chokepoints scattered throughout that can make the playing experience somewhat frustrating (namely the 27% and 45% waves), but they become fairly consistent over time and, as a result, less of an issue.


Overall, though, the gameplay is great, and it felt very tailored to my playstyle. The second half is much easier than the first, which is a huge win for those like me with poor nerve control. I'd definitely recommend this to anyone looking for a list demon to beat.

Bloodlust

I really wish I could've had a somewhat decent experience with this level given how good it is, but unfortunately any prospect of that happening at all was completely denied by some of the worst luck I've ever received, period. It became genuinely insufferable; I never once felt confident in my ability to finish it off, namely due to the chances of passing the extension being akin to trying to win big at the casino—something that never changed at all even after extensive practice, and also due to my consistency being wildly fluctuational—there'd be times where I wouldn't even get past the first half of the level for what felt like an eternity whilst I slowly continued to sink into a fused state of insanity and depression. I could genuinely sit here and rant for hours about how exhausted, stressed, anxious, nervous, depressed, etc. etc. this level made me feel, but I truly believe there are no words in the English language that can accurately depict and describe the relief I felt after finally making it over that last triple and seeing the endscreen. It's the first time I've ever been in pure disbelief after a completion.

CHROMA FINALE

This was extraordinarily close to being my worst experience in the entire game. The fact that this was even remotely comparable to Bloodlust in terms of luck is absolutely depressing, and what's even worse is that both of these were back to back completions. Obviously this is all in the mind and isn't at all rooted in reality, but I really thought I was owed at least an average experience with this level given what I had just gone through—but apparently not.


This level was recommended to me by a friend, and I finally decided to give it a shot on June 27th after returning from a three month break. This was primarily due to the potential it had to improve my wave and 4x speed skill, and secondarily because it seemed approachable (and maybe even fun!) considering its spot on the list. And, to my surprise, it began really well! I highly enjoyed it and was making quick progress, with runs seemingly just falling into my hands without much effort. By hour 10 and attempt 8,000—and this was very impressive for my standards—I felt ready to finish it off.


But of course, I clearly wasn't ready, because what ensued was another twelve hours of pure misery. Attempt after attempt was met by failure despite my consistency, and it ultimately just felt like I was gambling my life away, sitting and hoping like a loser in a casino who keeps praying for a windfall, plainly neglecting the fact that they're chasing a near impossibility but won't go home because they're too attached and have sadly fallen victim to the sunk cost fallacy. Each day that went by made me more and more desperate to just have it over and done with, but it was to no avail, because even when things were looking up, I just threw it all away every time I had a chance at winning.


Even when I put aside my woeful experience, CHROMA FINALE still isn't that great of a level. It's plagued by awful timings and just feels poorly playtested overall, with extremely awkward gameplay just left everywhere for seemingly no reason. It also has one massive chokepoint* in the section between the first and second wave that killed a ridiculous amount of runs, and speaking of this section, it's probably the worst offender for the aforementioned awkward gameplay. Absolutely cannot stand it. This is probably only worth playing if you're well above the skill level required to beat it, and even then, I wouldn't be surprised if it's still terrible.


* 58% x21, 59% x33 😑
